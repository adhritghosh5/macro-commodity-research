## 📑 10-Year Reform Timeline & Sectoral Winners/Losers

| Year | Policy Initiative | Sector Winners (Outperformed) | Laggards / Disrupted |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2014** | Make in India & Jan Dhan | Capital Goods, Defense, Electronics, Banking, Telecom | Import-heavy industries, Unorganized rural lenders |
| **2015** | Digital India Mission | Fintech, IT Services, Payment Processors | Paper-based verification & manual logistics |
| **2016** | Startup India, IBC, Demonetization | Private Banks, SaaS, E-Commerce, NBFCs | High-cash Real Estate, Unlisted Builders |
| **2017** | Goods & Services Tax (GST) & RERA | Formal FMCG, Auto, Organized Real Estate Developers | Unregistered MSMEs, High-end luxury, Coal |
| **2018** | Ayushman Bharat & Healthcare | Hospital Chains, Domestic Pharma, Medical Devices | Small non-compliant nursing homes |
| **2019** | Corporate Tax Rate Cut (to 22%/15%) | Auto, Oil & Gas, Cement, Heavy Manufacturing | Entities already on legacy concessional tax slabs |
| **2020** | PLI Schemes & Aatmanirbhar Stimulus | Electronics (Apple/Foxconn ecosystem), Chemicals, Telecom | Non-PLI legacy component manufacturers |
| **2021–22** | FDI Liberalization & 2nd Tax Wave | Private Defense, Insurance, Modern Retail | Capital-starved mid-sized domestic players |
| **2025** | GST 2.0 Slab Rationalization | Mass Consumption, Durable Goods, Logistics | High-tax bracket legacy friction goods |

## 🏦 Banking Equity Leadership Case Studies

The report details how the cleanup of the **Twin Balance Sheet Problem** catalyzed unprecedented compound earnings growth across premier Indian banks:

1. **HDFC Bank (Consistent Compounder & Mega-Merger):**
   * Sustained low gross NPAs and industry-leading NIMs throughout 2014–2023.
   * Executed the landmark merger with HDFC Ltd (effective July 1, 2023), consolidating the mortgage powerhouse and expanding balance sheet assets to over ₹36 Lakh Cr.
2. **ICICI Bank (Turnaround to Industry Benchmark):**
   * Post-2018 corporate governance restructuring, deep balance sheet provisioning, and aggressive pivoting towards high-margin retail and SME lending led to dramatic RoA expansion from ~0.4% in FY18 to >2.2% in FY24.
3. **State Bank of India (Consolidation & Scale):**
   * Merged 5 associate banks and Bharatiya Mahila Bank in April 2017. Benefited from massive treasury gains, IBC recoveries, and national distribution power.
4. **Axis Bank (Provisioning & Risk Calibration):**
   * Addressed wholesale book legacy slippages through accelerated provisioning, standardizing asset quality across retail and commercial verticals.
