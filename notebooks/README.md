# Interactive Research Notebooks

This directory contains Jupyter notebooks documenting exploratory data analysis, econometric tests, and visual demonstrations for each research theme.

## Notebook Catalog

### `01_crude_equities_analysis.ipynb`
* **Focus:** Crude oil price transmission to Indian oil marketing companies (IOC, BPCL, HPCL) and upstream producers (ONGC, OIL).
* **Techniques:** Vector Autoregressions (VAR), rolling correlation of Brent with Nifty Energy, crack spread regression, and EV adoption cross-elasticity.

### `02_ai_energy_commodity_stack.ipynb`
* **Focus:** Testing the 5-layer commodity scarcity thesis ($\text{AI} \to \text{Power} \to \text{Metals} \to \text{Nuclear} \to \text{Water}$).
* **Techniques:** Multi-asset standardization (Z-score normalization), lead-lag Granger causality between hyperscaler capex and industrial metal spot prices.

### `03_modi_policy_regime_shifts.ipynb`
* **Focus:** Quantitative event study on major economic policy announcements (2014–2025).
* **Techniques:** Cumulative Abnormal Return (CAR) calculations for banking and industrial sectors around key reform milestone dates (IBC, GST, Corporate Tax Cuts, PLI).
