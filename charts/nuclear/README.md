# Nuclear Power & SMR Infrastructure Visualizations

This directory contains visual exhibits detailing global and Indian nuclear power expansion, Small Modular Reactor (SMR) pipelines, and uranium market balances.

## Planned & Cataloged Charts
1. **`india_nuclear_capacity_2047_target.png`**: India's current 8.78 GW nuclear generation base vs. the 100 GW 2047 milestone trajectory.
2. **`smr_design_matrix.png`**: Technical comparison of India's indigenous BSMR-200, SMR-55, and HTGCR specifications vs global counterparts (NuScale, Westinghouse AP300).
3. **`uranium_supply_demand_deficit.png`**: Global primary uranium production vs. reactor requirements through 2035.
4. **`levelized_cost_firm_power.png`**: LCOE and Levelized Cost of Storage (LCOS) comparison: Nuclear SMRs vs. Solar + 8h Battery BESS.

### Source Data Feeds
* Department of Atomic Energy (DAE), Government of India
* Nuclear Power Corporation of India Limited (NPCIL)
