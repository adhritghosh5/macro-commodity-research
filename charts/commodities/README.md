# Industrial Commodities & Metals Visualizations

This directory contains analytical exhibits tracking industrial and strategic metal price cycles, inventories, and treatment charges.

## Planned & Cataloged Charts
1. **`indexed_metal_basket_2025_base.png`**: Indexed performance (April 2025 = 100) comparing Copper, Zinc, Tin, Silver, and Brent Crude.
2. **`lme_shfe_inventories.png`**: Days of consumption in global LME & SHFE warehouses for Copper and Zinc.
3. **`smelter_treatment_charges.png`**: Copper and Zinc smelter TC/RCs indicating upstream mine supply bottlenecks.
4. **`gold_silver_ratio.png`**: Long-term Gold/Silver ratio tracking industrial vs. monetary demand shifts.

### Source Data Feeds
* London Metal Exchange (LME)
* Shanghai Futures Exchange (SHFE)
* Fastmarkets / Wood Mackenzie Metals Intelligence
