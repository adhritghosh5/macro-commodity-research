# Crude Oil Visualizations & Price Action Charts

This directory contains visual exhibits, crack spread analysis, and price action charts for global crude benchmarks and Indian downstream margins.

## Planned & Cataloged Charts
1. **`brent_wti_price_action.png`**: Historical daily price trajectory of Brent and WTI crude, highlighting OPEC+ quota cuts and geopolitical events (Sep 2025).
2. **`omc_refining_margins.png`**: Gross Refining Margins (GRMs) vs. marketing margin spreads for IOC, BPCL, and HPCL.
3. **`india_crude_imports_breakdown.png`**: Breakdown of Indian crude import sources (Russia, Middle East, West Africa) and bilateral settlement currency share.
4. **`domestic_production_decline.png`**: India's domestic crude production trajectory showing the ~3% YoY structural contraction.

### Source Data Feeds
* U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA)
* Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC), Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, India
* Bloomberg / Reuters Energy Terminals
