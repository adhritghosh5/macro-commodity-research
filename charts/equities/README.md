# Equities & Sectoral Leadership Visualizations

This directory contains visual exhibits comparing Indian sectoral indices, banking stock performance, OMC financials, and EV pure-plays.

## Planned & Cataloged Charts
1. **`banking_leaders_relative_performance.png`**: Normalized equity total return of HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, and Axis Bank (2014–2025).
2. **`npa_recapitalization_trajectory.png`**: Gross Non-Performing Asset (GNPA) ratio collapse from 11.2% (2018) to <2.8% alongside PSU recapitalization infusions.
3. **`omc_operating_cash_flows.png`**: Comparative Free Cash Flow (FCF) and Operating Cash Flow trends for IOC vs BPCL vs HPCL.
4. **`ev_vs_omc_divergence.png`**: Price performance of EV manufacturers (Ather Energy, Ola Electric) against upstream and downstream oil baskets.

### Source Data Feeds
* National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) / BSE India
* Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Financial Stability Reports
* S&P Capital IQ / Company Filings (Annual Reports)
