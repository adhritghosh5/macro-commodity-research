# AI Energy & Physical Infrastructure Charts

This directory contains analytical exhibits modeling hyperscaler data centre power demand, grid interconnection queues, cooling intensity, and water stress.

## Planned & Cataloged Charts
1. **`datacenter_electricity_projections.png`**: Global and Indian data centre electricity consumption (TWh) projections (2024–2035).
2. **`grid_interconnection_bottleneck.png`**: Average wait times and transmission queue capacity in GW across major regional grids.
3. **`water_cooling_intensity_by_architecture.png`**: Water consumption effectiveness (WCE) across Direct-to-Chip Liquid Cooling, Evaporative, and Immersion systems.
4. **`nqh2o_water_futures_price_action.png`**: Historical price trajectory of the CME Nasdaq Veles California Water Index (NQH2O) and seasonal drought correlation.

### Source Data Feeds
* International Energy Agency (IEA) Electricity Reports
* Goldman Sachs / Morgan Stanley Technology Research
* CME Group (NQH2O contract data)
* Central Electricity Authority (CEA) of India
