# Quantitative Backtests & Portfolio Strategies

This directory houses the quantitative backtesting harnesses, factor attribution models, and performance tear sheets for strategies developed across the research notes.

## Backtesting Strategies

1. **[`commodity_scarcity_backtest.py`](file:///c:/Users/adhri/Downloads/Research%20Portfolio/macro-commodity-research/backtests/commodity_scarcity_backtest.py):**
   * Long/short commodity and power equipment equities allocation guided by the 5-layer composite scarcity signal $S_t$.
   * Computes annualized Sharpe ratio, transaction cost drag, maximum drawdown, and Calmar ratio.

2. **[`policy_momentum_backtest.py`](file:///c:/Users/adhri/Downloads/Research%20Portfolio/macro-commodity-research/backtests/policy_momentum_backtest.py):**
   * Regime-switching equity strategy capitalizing on Indian policy waves (Make in India, IBC/Recapitalization, PLI schemes, and GST rationalization).
